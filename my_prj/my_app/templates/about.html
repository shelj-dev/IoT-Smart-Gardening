What is Smart Gardening?

Smart Gardening is an IoT-powered ecosystem designed to bring intelligence to your plant care routine. By combining advanced sensor technology with real-time web monitoring, we enable you to maintain your garden from anywhere in the world.

Whether you are a hobbyist with indoor plants or managing a larger home garden, our platform ensures your plants receive the exact hydration they need, preventing both drying and root rot through precise automation.

Real-time Dashboard

Instantly monitor soil moisture levels, pump status, and system health through our live-updating dashboard. No manual checks required.

Automatic Hydration

Set a moisture threshold and let our system handle the rest. The pump triggers automatically when your soil gets too dry, maintaining an optimal environment.

Smart Delays

Our "Hydration Delay" feature prevents over-watering by ensuring the system waits for a defined period after a successful watering cycle.

Manual Override

Need to water your plants right now? Use our dedicated manual watering page to trigger the pump unconditionally at any time.
